# SUSPECT_CAST.md

## 1. Document purpose

This document defines the canonical first roster of the 10 main criminal suspects for **Deolane San Paolo / `DeolaneSanPaolo`**.

It is the source of truth for:

- suspect names;
- suspect IDs;
- visual identity;
- dossier flavor;
- generation guidance for portrait art;
- encounter-art consistency;
- warrant-relevant trait direction.

These suspects must be rendered in the mandatory visual style defined by `VISUAL_SPEC.md`:
late-1980s/early-1990s IBM PC DOS, low-resolution caricature portrait style, strong outlines, limited palette, hard shading, no antialiasing.

---

## 2. Roster overview

There are 10 core suspects:

### Women
1. Deolane San Paolo
2. Cris Minosa
3. Lua Metrayu
4. Thais Kizita
5. Tigrínia Fonseca

### Men
6. Narcola Tamacho
7. Paulo Escolar
8. Zezé do Rap
9. Cleitinho Matador
10. Vanzeira

---

## 3. Suspect format

Each suspect entry defines:

- `id`

## 4.1 Deolane San Paolo

- **id:** `deolane-san-paolo`
- **role:** mastermind / final villain / primary antagonist

### Core visual identity
Deolane San Paolo is the central criminal icon of the game.  
She is glamorous, exaggerated, ostentatious and instantly recognizable.

She must always appear as:

- blonde;
- with extremely large exaggerated lips;
- wearing intense bright red lipstick;
- heavy, forced, excessive makeup;
- large earrings;
- a large gold necklace;
- an enormous gold pendant;
- visually flashy, vain and intimidating.

### Personality / vibe
She projects luxury, arrogance, vanity and criminal celebrity energy.
She should feel like someone who always wants to be the center of attention, even while being hunted across the globe.

### Generation notes
Her portrait must be the most memorable in the cast.
At dossier size, the first things the player notices should be:

1. the lips;
2. the blonde hair;
3. the heavy makeup;
4. the gold jewelry.

She must look like the undisputed queen of the criminal network.

### Warrant-trait direction
- **sex:** female
- **hair:** blonde
- **hobby:** luxury collecting / high society leisure
- **feature:** huge red lips
- **vehicle:** luxury car or private jet aesthetic

## 4.2 Cris Minosa

- **id:** `cris-minosa`
- **role:** senior operative

### Core visual identity
Cris Minosa is a dark, stylish, dangerous-looking woman with black curly hair.
She should feel elegant but severe, with a slightly sinister charisma.

Visual anchors:

- black curly hair with strong volume;
- sharp eyes;
- darker, more restrained makeup than Deolane;
- narrower face;
- expressive cheekbones;
- fashionable but dangerous appearance.

### Personality / vibe
Cold, calculating and stylish.
She feels like someone who speaks little, observes everything and leaves no loose ends.

### Generation notes
Her silhouette should be dominated by the curly dark hair.
She should contrast Deolane by looking less flashy and more controlled.
Her expression should feel intelligent, suspicious and difficult to trust.

### Warrant-trait direction
- **sex:** female
- **hair:** black curly hair
- **hobby:** rare perfume collecting or card games
- **feature:** sharp cheekbones / intense eyes
- **vehicle:** black sports coupe or motorcycle

## 4.3 Lua Metrayu

- **id:** `lua-metrayu`
- **role:** mafia-linked transnational operative

### Core visual identity
Lua Metrayu is an Asian criminal figure associated with organized crime aesthetics.
She should look poised, elegant, dangerous and quietly powerful.

Visual anchors:

- East Asian features;
- straight dark hair, long and sleek;
- refined but intimidating expression;
- expensive outfit with a criminal-underworld vibe;
- subtle jewelry, more precise than flashy;

She should feel more “mafia elegance” than “street criminal.”

### Warrant-trait direction
- **sex:** female

- **hobby:** mahjong / strategy games / art dealing
- **feature:** cold expression / narrow eyes / refined posture
- **vehicle:** black sedan or executive limousine

## 5. Cast design rules

### 5.1 Distinct silhouette rule
Each suspect must be identifiable at a glance through at least two strong visual anchors.

### 5.2 No duplicates
No two suspects should feel visually interchangeable.
Hair, face shape, expression, accessories and vibe must separate them clearly.

### 5.3 DOS caricature rule
All suspects must be rendered as exaggerated low-resolution caricatures in the original target style.
They must not become realistic portraits or modern HD pixel-art characters.

### 5.4 Warrant clarity rule
Each suspect must be compatible with the five warrant-relevant identity categories:
- sex
- hair
- hobby
- feature
- vehicle

The final suspect matrix must allow unique filtering in gameplay.

The canonical balanced runtime matrix is:

| Suspect | Sex | Hair | Hobby | Feature | Vehicle |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Deolane | female | straight blonde | high society | exaggerated jewelry | luxury car |
| Cris | female | curly black | collecting | intense gaze | motorcycle |
| Lua | female | straight black | collecting | intense gaze | limousine |
| Thais | female | chestnut | high society | beauty mark | convertible |
| Tigrínia | female | straight blonde | betting | smug smile | convertible |
| Narcola | male | balding | betting | huge nose | cargo van |
| Paulo | male | black | music | large mustache | pickup truck |
| Zezé | male | black | music | sunglasses | coupe |
| Cleitinho | male | shaved | collecting | intense gaze | motorcycle |
| Vanzeira | male | shaved | parties | exaggerated jewelry | luxury car |

This expanded matrix restores the cast-specific detail of the earlier warrant directions while retaining shared evidence across sex, hair, hobby, feature and vehicle. Some distinctive values are isolated strong discriminators, but generation makes those clues uncommon, uses no more than one per case and presents shared traits first. Multi-trait intersections identify all ten suspects.

### 5.5 Deolane supremacy rule
Deolane is the iconic central villain.
She must remain the most visually dominant and memorable suspect in the roster.
